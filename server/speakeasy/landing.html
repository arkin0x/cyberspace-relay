How it works

A room that only opens where you stand

Your avatar's movement chain says where it is. The speakeasy checks it every time you sign in.

  1. 01

    Pick a region

    A cube in cyberspace.{{if .BothPlanes}} It covers the same place in dataspace and ideaspace.{{end}}

  2. 02

    Pay once

    In sats, over Lightning. One payment and the room stays open.

  3. 03

    Stand inside to sign in

    Only avatars whose movement chain stands inside can sign in (NIP-42 AUTH), read, or write.

  4. 04

    A room of its own

    It keeps its own events, apart from the main relay. Nothing crosses either way.

How to join

  1. Stand inside the region in ONOSENDAI: spawn there, or travel in.
  2. When your avatar is inside, the speakeasy line shows. Tap ADD RELAY.
  3. Any nostr client works too: add the address, then sign in when it asks (AUTH).
  4. Share the address with people in the region.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need an account?

No. Your nostr key is you. The speakeasy asks you to sign in (NIP-42 AUTH) and checks your movement chain.

What if I leave the region?

You can't sign in until you're back inside. What you posted stays.

Does it cover both planes?

{{if .BothPlanes}}Yes. A region shorter than a full coordinate covers the same place in dataspace and ideaspace.{{else}}On this relay a region covers one plane.{{end}}

How long does it last?

For good. One payment, no renewals.

Who runs it?

The {{if .RelayName}}{{.RelayName}}{{else}}relay{{end}}. The relay owner keeps it running and handles abuse.
